How it works

From connect to intelligent in 10 minutes.

No infrastructure to provision. No ML pipelines to maintain. Plug in your repos and cloud accounts, and CodeIQ starts shipping value on day one.

01
Connect your stack
OAuth-based integrations with GitHub, GitLab, AWS, GCP, Slack, and Jira. Read-only by default, with explicit approval for any write actions.
02
CodeIQ learns your codebase
Ingests your repo history, coding conventions, incident runbooks, and architecture docs to build a context-aware model of your engineering org.
03
AI works 24/7
Reviews PRs as they open, monitors infra continuously, and stands ready as a first responder when incidents trigger. Your team gets notified only when input is needed.
04
Teams improve over time
Weekly engineering health reports surface trends. CodeIQ learns what matters to your team, reducing noise and increasing signal with every sprint.
